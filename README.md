## ✨ What it does

- 📍 **One-click Teleport** — click the map, search a place (address autocomplete), paste coordinates or a Plus Code, teleport from Street View (Pro), or spin a 3D globe and click to land.
- 🕹️ **Live Joystick walk** — a four-direction pad (north, east, south, west, plus stop) to move in real time at walk, bike or drive speed. The arrow keys also nudge the pin.
- 🛣️ **Routes** — a two-stop route follows a road route you preview first (the free plan includes 3 routes a month). **Multi-stop routes** _(Pro)_ follow roads at a realistic pace when Realistic ETA is on, and otherwise go straight between your stops. Both have smooth turns and optional GPS drift, and multi-stop routes can loop.
- 🚗 **Transport modes** — travel a route by **Driving, Walking, Cycling, Public transport** (moves between your stops at about 40 km/h, with no station stops; with Realistic ETA on, it follows the car route at driving pace), **Boat** (a great-circle line through your stops at boat speed; it does not avoid land), or **Plane** (a great-circle flight path from your first stop to your last). *(Simulated in-flight altitude is Android-only; desktop flies the path without altitude.)*
- ✈️ **Flight planner** — pick two airports (searchable), optionally choose from sample departure times, then fly the great-circle route. Uses a bundled airport list, so no API is needed.
- 🔀 **Drag-to-reorder** multi-stop waypoints, plus one-tap **Reverse route**.
- 🎮 **Games mode** _(Pro)_ — when you jump to a spot from its list, a **timer** shows a suggested wait based on how far you moved, with an optional lock until it ends, plus presets for four location-based games. A free, read-only board of current in-game events sits in the side panel. Games that check whether a location is simulated may not work from Wander Desktop, and Wander can't stop a game from warning or banning your account.
- 🌍 **3D globe, Street View teleport (Pro) & satellite maps** — line up the exact spot before you go.
- 🕒 **Local time** at the location you set, with a heads-up when your computer's time zone doesn't match it, and an **IP card** explaining that apps can also estimate where you are from your IP address.
- 🔎 **AI natural-language teleport** ("take me to Times Square") and an **AI day planner** that suggests a day of stops _(Pro)_.
- ⭐ **Favorites** with **folders, tags & notes**, plus **teleport history** and saved loops _(Pro)_. In the app window these are cleared each time Wander restarts.
- 📂 **GPX & KML import** and **restore** from a backup file.
- 🖥️ **Multi-device _(beta)_** — hold one location across several connected iPhones at once.
- 🌙 **Dark Mode** and a language picker with **10 languages** (English plus 9 translations of the main card titles; buttons and messages stay in English).
- 🔌 **iPhone over USB** — plug in and go. After you've paired once over USB, you can also connect over Wi-Fi.
- 🔓 **No jailbreak.** Works on a normal iPhone with Developer Mode turned on.
- 💻 **Cross-platform** — macOS and Windows.

Start on the **free plan**: 1 teleport every day, plus 15 minutes of joystick and 3 routes every month. **Wander Pro** ($3.99 a month, $36 a year, or $80 once) removes those limits and unlocks the other movement modes (Jump, Multi-Spot and games mode), Street View, saved loops, record & replay, the itinerary queue, scheduling, the auto-stop zone and the AI features.

> **Mobile-only:** downloadable offline maps are in the **iPhone & Android** apps. The desktop map loads its tiles over the internet. See the whole family at [wanderspoofer.com](https://wanderspoofer.com).

## 🧑‍💻 Run from source

For developers who'd rather build it themselves:

```bash
cd wander-desktop
./run.sh          # first run creates a venv, installs deps, and launches
```

On macOS, `run.sh` starts the app with `sudo`, because creating the device tunnel needs admin rights. The map opens in its own app window (or in your browser if the window can't start).

**Manual alternative (macOS):**

```bash
python3 -m venv .venv
source .venv/bin/activate
pip install -r requirements.txt
sudo ./.venv/bin/python src/main.py
```

On Windows: `py -3.11 -m venv .venv`, `.venv\Scripts\activate`, `pip install -r requirements.txt`, then `python src\main.py`. The app asks for admin through UAC.

**Requirements**
- **macOS** or **Windows**
- **Python 3.11** (the version the release builds use; `run.sh` builds its venv with whatever `python3` is on your PATH, so make sure that is 3.11)
- iPhone with **Developer Mode** enabled (Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode)
- Admin rights (the device tunnel needs `sudo` on macOS / UAC on Windows)

## ⚙️ How it works

A local **Flask** server hosts a **Leaflet** map (`src/templates/map.html`) and talks to the iPhone through **pymobiledevice3**: it starts a QUIC/TCP tunnel, mounts the Developer Disk Image, and sets location via the DVT `LocationSimulation` instrument. Teleporting is a `POST /wander/teleport {lat,lng}`. Walk and route modes keep one location session open and move the position along the path, usually about once a second.
